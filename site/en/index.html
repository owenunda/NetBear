A bear in your tray that runs with your internet

NetBear lives next to the Windows clock. It runs faster the faster you download, stands still when there's no traffic and hibernates when you go offline.

Download for Windows View the code

Free · Windows 10 and 11 · Open source (MIT)

Three states, nothing more

Every second NetBear measures the bytes going through your network adapters and picks how the bear moves. Hover over it to see download and upload speed.

  • Runs

    There's traffic. The faster you download, the faster it runs.

    from 10 KB/s

  • Stands still

    Barely any traffic. It waits and blinks now and then.

    under 10 KB/s

  • Hibernates

    No adapter has a default gateway, so there's no connection.

    offline

Built to stay out of the way

One tray icon and a menu with three options. No windows, no accounts, nothing to set up.
